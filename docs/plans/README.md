# Plans

This folder holds plans and investigations with open work. Everything else in
`docs/` describes how CloudMus works now.

Once a plan is carried out, what it changed goes into the document for that
part of the app, and the plan is shortened to what's left or deleted.

- [`mpv.md`](mpv.md): a libmpv of our own on every platform: smooth fades,
  equalizer and effects, visualization, crossfade, OpenSL ES's audio session.
- [`music-videos.md`](music-videos.md): showing a track's video from YouTube
  Music or Yandex Music where there is one.
- [`android-battery.md`](android-battery.md): what background playback costs
  the battery on Android, and ideas to make it cost less.
- [`gwm-head-unit.md`](gwm-head-unit.md): GWM head units: the volume
  slider that can't change the unit's fixed volume, and storage access past
  GWM's own permission pages.
- [`go-backends.md`](go-backends.md): backends in Go instead of Python
  (evaluated, not started).
